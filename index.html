BEFORE
YOU BUY.

Are the 12-week gym programs suitable for beginners?

Yes. The first three weeks focus on learning the movements with light weights. Every exercise has a coaching cue and a machine-friendly swap.

Does each program include an Indian vegetarian meal plan?

Yes. Every program includes a seven-day vegetarian plan and a separate non-veg plan. The guide shows you how to reach your protein target with foods such as soya, paneer and curd, plus optional protein powder.

Is there a three-day full-body or four-day upper/lower gym split?

Each program includes a four-day upper/lower split and a three-day full-body split that follow the same 12-week roadmap.

How do I choose between fat loss, body recomp and muscle gain?

Choose Fat Loss if getting leaner is your main goal. Choose Body Recomp if you want to build muscle while working on fat loss. Choose Muscle Gain if adding size is your priority and you want a lean-bulk plan.

Which guide combines a lean bulk with Indian vegetarian meals?

Muscle Gain pairs progressive lifting with higher-calorie Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus. It includes guidance on when to adjust your food intake as your weight changes.

How much do the programs cost?

Each 12-week PDF guide costs ₹649. The Complete Set includes Fat Loss, Body Recomp and Muscle Gain for ₹1,299.

Can I do it at home?

The programs are designed for a gym. Each guide includes an exercise substitution table for equipment you’re missing.

How do I buy a guide and get the PDF?

Choose Buy now on your guide or bundle to open Razorpay checkout. Once your payment is confirmed, share the payment reference with Aryan on WhatsApp for PDF delivery. You can also message Aryan if checkout is unavailable.

What is the refund policy?

All sales are final once the digital files are delivered. If a file is damaged or won’t open, message Aryan for a working copy.
